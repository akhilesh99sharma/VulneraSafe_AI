Chapter 1 · The Worry

It starts with a question no one can answer in time

"Somewhere in next month's shipments, a pathogen risk is rising. Where should my team look first — and what do I do when I find it?"

— the Food Safety Operations Director of a regional grocery chain

Why it's hard

  • The warning signs are real but scattered across agencies
  • Inspection, testing and supplier budgets are finite
  • Acting late means contaminated product reaches shelves

Salmonella, Listeria and STEC send hundreds of thousands of people to hospital every year — and they hit the most vulnerable hardest. The data to see it coming exists. It just never becomes a signal anyone can act on.

Chapter 1 · The Stakes

Vulnerability isn't evenly spread

Pregnancy & newborns

Listeria in ready-to-eat foods can cause miscarriage, stillbirth and severe newborn infection.

Children under 5

Higher rates of severe STEC complications and Salmonella hospitalization.

Adults 65+

Weakened immunity drives higher hospitalization and death across all three pathogens.

So the real question isn't just where is risk rising — it's where does rising risk meet the people who can least absorb it. That reframing shapes everything that follows.

Chapter 3 · Observe

Five One Health pathways, joined with their real limits

🧪 Food / animal

Primary. USDA FSIS establishment lab sampling — the surveillance backbone.

🌦️ Environment

NOAA state-month climate. A small, honestly-reported lift — not a headline driver.

🧑‍⚕️ Human health

CDC NORS outbreaks, lagged 365 days — historical corroboration, not a live feature.

🥫 Food security

US Census ACS + USDA Food Access Atlas — community vulnerability & access context.

🚚 Supply chain

A clearly-labeled SIMULATED retailer network — no real retail data exists.

📋 Regulatory recalls

Real openFDA food-enforcement recalls as independent regulatory evidence.

The honesty is the point: where a pathway is thin or blocked, we label it a gap rather than dress it up. Every value carries source, date, license and checksum.
