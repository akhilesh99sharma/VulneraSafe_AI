A multi-pathogen, One Health food-safety early-warning & decision-support platform

Vulnerability-aware surveillance risk forecasting for a national grocery retailer's Food Safety Operations Director — reproducible analysis + a static React decision-support web app on GitHub Pages.

The Problem

Foodborne hazard signals are scattered — and attention is finite

  • Human illness, food/animal testing, weather, supply chain and community-vulnerability signals live in separate silos and are rarely read together.
  • A retail food-safety team has a fixed inspection, lot-testing and supplier-documentation budget each period and must decide where to point it.
  • Acting too late risks distributing contaminated product; acting everywhere is impossible.

The decision we support

Where to direct next period's finite inspection, lot-testing and supplier-scrutiny effort to reduce the chance of distributing contaminated product — given predicted surveillance risk.

Decision support only. Not a regulatory determination; it never declares a product safe or unsafe.

VulneraSafe AI04
Predictive Modeling · Task 2

Forecast elevated surveillance risk — only where data supports it

Method

  • Target: binary "elevated next-period positivity" at pathogen × commodity × region × time, fixed before tuning
  • Baselines: seasonal-naive + regularized logistic regression
  • Integrated: LightGBM gradient boosting + Random Forest on 42 leakage-safe features
  • Feasibility gating: a pathogen predicts only if positives, coverage & calibration support it — otherwise it shows historical surveillance with uncertainty

Honest validation

  • Strict chronological split — all train periods precede all test periods
  • Rolling-origin validation; most-recent ~20% held untouched
  • Rare-event metrics: PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, Brier, calibration
  • No feature uses information after the horizon cutoff — leakage checks enforced

VulneraSafe AI07
Operational Decision Support · Task 3

Risk becomes safety-constrained action

Six recommended actions

  • Prioritize additional testing / inspection
  • Request supplier documentation & review
  • Escalate concerning shipments for human assessment
  • Match lots against confirmed recalls
  • Isolate inventory under confirmed recall
  • Allocate compliant replacement inventory for access

Three policies compared

  • A — business as usual
  • B — model-guided safety prioritization
  • C — model-guided safety + food-access-aware optimization

Optimization under hard safety constraints. No secondary gain (cost, availability, access) can ever override a food-safety rule.

Labeling & Provenance

Every value knows what it is

OBSERVED

Recorded from a real empirical source — surveillance, testing, weather, demographics.

PREDICTED

Produced by a validated model for a future period, shown with uncertainty.

SIMULATED

From the documented synthetic operational network — never a real transaction.

  • Maps labeled with pathogen, geography, period, model/surveillance status, uncertainty and data freshness — no blank fields.
  • Orange & red reserved strictly for active alert conditions. Scientific light theme, navy & teal accents.
  • No decorative metrics — if it isn't observed, predicted or simulated, it isn't shown.
VulneraSafe AI10
Responsible Interpretation

Decision support that refuses to overclaim

  • Never infers contamination from a model alone, and never declares a product safe or unsafe from predicted risk.
  • Never infers causal outbreak linkage from genomic or temporal similarity alone.
  • Verified official recalls are shown as regulatory evidence, visually distinct from model alerts — and never suppressed for missing model uncertainty.
  • Model recommendations are withheld if required evidence or quantified uncertainty is unavailable.
  • Recommendations are tested for whether they systematically disadvantage high-need communities, with the measured disparity shown as pass/fail.
  • High-consequence actions are blocked pending human review.
